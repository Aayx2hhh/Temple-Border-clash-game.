Thailand vs Cambodia · Preah Vihear Conflict

Temple Border Clash

Objective: Control temples and crush enemy resources before ceasefire.

Setup: Each player starts with 3 Infantry on HQ, 5 Power, 5 Supply, and 1 Chance card.

Phases:

  • Resource Phase: Gain 1 Power per claimed temple, or 1 Supply if none controlled.
  • Action Phase: Choose 1 action (Deploy, Claim, Attack, Repair, or play a card).

Deploy cost (Supply): HQ→A = 2, A→B = 1, B→C = 1 (mirrored for Blue)
